# 简体中文

## 1. 关于回想

回想（Echo）由 Shutian Ma 提供，帮助你整理网页链接，并从自己的收藏中获得行动灵感。

无需注册 Echo 账号。

本政策适用于 iPhone App 及其分享扩展。

## 2. 保存在设备上的信息

回想会在设备上保存你主动添加的链接、可获取的网页标题和简介、网页中提到的地点、分类、收藏及删除时间、提醒设置，以及用于减少重复推荐的本地记录。

主 App 和分享扩展通过设备上的 iOS App Group 共享这些数据。

回想不运营用于保存你的收藏的服务器，也不提供自己的云同步。分类和建议使用本地规则与模板生成。

回想不集成广告、第三方使用统计或第三方崩溃上报 SDK，不将你的数据用于广告追踪。

## 3. 网页链接与第三方平台

导入或查看链接时，回想可能访问原网站及其重定向目标，获取公开网页信息。

目标网站会接收正常网络请求所需的信息，可能包括访问的 URL、IP 地址和浏览器或设备请求头。回想也可能使用 Apple 的链接预览和浏览器框架。

你在 App 内或其他浏览器打开的网页，可能依据其自身隐私政策使用 Cookie、本地网页存储、统计和登录功能。

如果你主动在原网站登录，登录由该网站与系统浏览器组件处理。回想不会要求你向开发者提供其他平台的密码。

第三方网站的数据处理方式由其各自负责。

打开 Apple 地图链接时，由 Apple 地图或浏览器处理，适用 Apple 的相关政策。

## 4. 系统权限与剪贴板

回想仅在你开启提醒时请求系统通知权限，通过 iOS 在本机安排提醒。

链接收藏功能不请求设备实时位置、通讯录、相册或相机权限。

推荐中使用的地点来自收藏网页，而非你的实时位置。

回想只接收你主动粘贴或分享的链接，不持续监控剪贴板，不读取你的微信收藏夹，也不自动同步其他 App 的历史收藏。

## 5. 备份、回收箱与删除

你可以通过系统文件选择器导出备份。

备份文件包含收藏、分类和回收箱内容，保存在你选择的位置。

若选择 iCloud Drive 或其他文件服务，适用相应服务的政策。iOS 设备备份也可能根据系统设置包含 App 数据。

删除的收藏保留在回收箱，直到你恢复或永久删除。

永久删除不会自动删除你以前导出的文件或系统备份，请自行管理这些副本。

删除 App 可以移除其本地数据，但仍受 iOS 备份和恢复机制影响。

## 6. 联系支持与本网站

联系支持时，我们会收到你主动提供的邮箱、消息及可选截图，用于答复和处理问题。

请勿发送密码；非必要时无需提供私人链接。

发布在公开 GitHub Issue 中的信息是公开的。

本政策网站托管在 GitHub Pages，GitHub 可能根据其政策，为托管和安全处理访问者的网络信息。

这些页面未添加广告、统计脚本或追踪 Cookie。

数据处理方式变化时，我们可能更新本政策并修改上方日期。

# English

## 1. About Echo

Echo (回想), provided by Shutian Ma , helps you organize web links and rediscover ideas from your own collection.

You do not need an Echo account.

This policy applies to the iPhone app and its share extension.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

Echo stores links you choose to add, page titles and descriptions when available, places mentioned on those pages, categories, saved and deleted dates, reminder preferences, and local records used to reduce repeated suggestions.

The app and its share extension share this information through an iOS App Group on your device.

Echo does not operate a server for your collection or provide its own cloud sync. Classification and suggestions use local rules and templates.

We do not integrate advertising, third-party analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDKs, and do not use your data for advertising tracking.

## 3. Web Links and Third-Party Websites

When you import or revisit a link, Echo may contact the original website and its redirect destinations to retrieve public page information.

The destination receives normal network-request information, which may include the requested URL, your IP address and browser or device request headers. Apple's link-preview and browser frameworks may also be used.

If you open a website in the app or another browser, that website may use cookies, local web storage, analytics or login features under its own privacy policy.

If you choose to sign in on an original website, its sign-in process is handled by that website and the system browser components.

Echo does not ask you to give platform passwords to the developer.

We do not control third-party websites' data practices.

Opening an Apple Maps link is handled by Apple Maps or the browser and is subject to Apple's policies.

## 4. Permissions and Clipboard

Echo asks for notification permission only when you enable reminders.

Reminders are scheduled locally using iOS.

Echo does not request your current device location, contacts, photo library or camera for its link-saving features.

Places used in suggestions come from the links you saved, not live device location.

The app accepts links you paste or explicitly share to Echo.

It does not continuously monitor your clipboard, read your WeChat favorites, or automatically import other apps' saved collections.

## 5. Backup, Trash and Deletion

You may export a backup using the system file picker.

The file contains your collection, categories and Trash records, and is saved to the location you select.

If you select iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider's terms apply.

iOS device backups may also include app data according to your system settings.

Deleted saves remain in Trash until you restore or permanently delete them.

Permanently deleting a save removes the app's Trash copy but does not remove previously exported files or system backups.

Manage those separately.

You can remove the app's local data by deleting the app, subject to iOS backup and restore behavior.

## 6. Support and This Website

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message and optional screenshots.

We use it to respond to your request and resolve issues.

Do not include passwords or private links unless needed.

Information you post in public GitHub issues is public.

This policy site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

GitHub may process visitor network information for hosting and security under its own policies.

These pages do not add advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies.

## 7. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change and will update the date above.
