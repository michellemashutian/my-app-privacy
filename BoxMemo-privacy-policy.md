## English

### Data on your device

Boxes, locations, items, tags, and photos are stored in the app’s storage on this device. BoxFinder has no account system, does not upload this content to developer-operated servers, and includes no advertising or tracking analytics.

### Photo recognition

Apple Vision processes photos on your device. Photos are not sent to BoxFinder or third-party AI services. Suggested tags are added to a record only when you select them and save.

### Camera and Photos permissions

Camera access lets you take photos. Photos access lets you choose photos or save generated label images. Permissions are requested when these features are used. You can manage them in system Settings and can enter items manually without granting access.

### Exporting and sharing

You choose when to share or save a label image. Exported images may contain a box name and ID and are saved to the Photos library, file location, or other app you select.

### Deleting records

Deleting a box removes its item records and associated photos from the app. Label images already exported to Photos, Files, or other apps remain there until you delete them separately.

### Backups and device changes

BoxFinder does not currently provide its own cloud sync or backup and restore feature. App data may be included in Apple device backups and restores depending on your system settings. Uninstalling the app may remove your local records.

### Contact

When you email support, we receive the email address and message you choose to provide and use them to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive photos or information. We cannot remotely access the box records on your device.

### 数据保存在设备上

盒子、位置、物品、标签和照片保存在此设备的 App 存储空间中。盒物记 没有账户系统，不向开发者服务器上传这些内容，也不包含广告或跟踪分析服务。

### 照片识别

识别使用 Apple Vision 在设备上处理照片，不发送到 盒物记 或第三方 AI 服务。推荐标签只有在你点选并保存后才会加入记录。

### 照片与相机权限

相机用于拍摄照片；相册用于选择照片，或保存你生成的标签图片。相应功能使用时才会请求权限。你可以在系统设置中管理权限，不授权也可以手动记录物品。

### 导出与分享

分享或保存标签图片由你主动发起。导出的图片可能包含盒子名称和编号，并由你选择的相册、文件位置或其他 App 保存。

### 删除记录

删除盒子会删除其中的物品记录和 App 内关联照片。已导出到相册、文件或其他 App 的标签图片不会随之删除，需要在对应位置单独删除。

### 备份与换机

盒物记 尚不提供独立的云同步或备份恢复功能。App 数据可能随你开启的 Apple 系统设备备份一起保存或恢复，具体取决于系统设置。卸载 App 可能导致本机记录丢失。

通过邮件联系支持时，我们会收到你主动提供的邮箱地址和邮件内容，仅用于回复和处理请求。请勿发送敏感照片或信息。我们无法远程访问你设备上的盒子记录。
