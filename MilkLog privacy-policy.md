**English — Privacy Policy**

**MilkLog** respects your privacy.

Your baby feeding and milk intake records are stored locally on your device. MilkLog does not collect these records or transmit them to the developer’s servers.

If you choose to export your records as a CSV file, the exported data will be saved or shared to the destination you select. Data may also be included in device backups, depending on your system settings.

MilkLog does not use analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

No account or sign-in is required to use the app.

MilkLog offers an optional in-app purchase to support the developer. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and are subject to Apple’s privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment card details.
